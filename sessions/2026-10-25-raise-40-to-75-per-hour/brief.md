# Brief: ask CEO and COO to raise my pay from $40 to $75 per hour

- Deadline: none stated. Assume the ask happens in the next two to four weeks (assumed).

## Success and horizon

## Which way the founder is leaning
Ask for $75 now. Fallback if refused: $75 with a lower base (Option B). The council tests this lean.
