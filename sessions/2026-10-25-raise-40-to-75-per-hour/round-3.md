## Chair
- **Value floor ($156k vs $200k vs $219k):** adopted in part. Buffett's point that $156k only pays for the hours and leaves no margin of safety went unanswered, so the floor to ask for $75 is $200k, each line confirmed by the COO. Hormozi's $219k (3:1) goes in the minority report. Between $156k and $200k, open on C but expect a lower day-90 target.
- **13 weeks of trend (Wickman):** adopted, as "up to 13 weeks of log data per figure".
- **CEO and COO both initial the targets (Wickman, Leila):** adopted.
- **Runbooks exist before the meeting (Leila):** adopted; the day-30 test where someone else runs one system stays.
- **Targets as logged inputs, no revenue-lift line (Hormozi):** adopted for the day-90 pass test. Revenue lift stays on the proof sheet only where the COO accepts the attribution.
- **B fallback terms (Naval, Elon, Hormozi, Buffett):** adopted. B is a last resort, base at least $60, one metric the COO already reports, floor and cap, day-90 conversion to base, and if no signed formula by day 30 the fixed step-up stands.
- **Leverage test (Elon):** adopted. If rebuilding the systems would take under a month of work, open on C, not flat.
- **Know-how rights clause (Naval):** adopted in part, as a request for credit and documentation, not rights. Check the employment agreement first; most employers own work done on the job.
