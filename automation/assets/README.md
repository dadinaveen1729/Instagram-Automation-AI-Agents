# Assets (supplied by Boost Oxygen, never generated)

- `logo/logo-reverse.png`   approved reverse logo (white letters) for dark green backgrounds
- `logo/logo-fullcolor.png` approved full-color logo for white or light gray backgrounds
  Only the approved logo files from the Brand Toolkit. No edits, recolors, or redraws. If these
  files are missing, posts are rendered without a logo and flagged for review.
- `approved/` approved product and lifestyle photos, plus `approved/catalog.json`:
  `[{"id": "sport-orange-large", "file": "sport-orange-large.jpg", "alt": "...", "contains_product": true}]`
  Product canisters are never generated or recreated by AI. Photos are placed whole: no crop,
  no filter, no overlay. Photos in the toolkit PDF are Getty or illustrative, and licensing must
  be secured before use.
- `fonts/` Oswald SemiBold and Open Sans (SIL Open Font License), the toolkit's typefaces.
