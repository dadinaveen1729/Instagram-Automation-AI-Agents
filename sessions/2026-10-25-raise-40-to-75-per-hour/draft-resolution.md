# Draft resolution: raise from $40 to $75 per hour

**Recommendation: Option C, framed as an ask for $75.** The founder says "I'm asking for $75 an hour" and brings the proof sheet. The path is $55 from the next pay period and $75 automatically at day 90, against three targets the COO signs before $55 starts. $55 is never the headline. Verdict label: GO WITH CONDITIONS.

Fallback: if the CEO or COO calls the 90-day gate a stall or will not fund a fixed $75, switch in the room to Option B: a base of at least $60 plus a bonus on one logged metric, capped, signed on one page before month one, paid quarterly, with total pay at or above $75/hr effective (Buffett, Naval). If the proof sheet clears $300k a year, open flat at $75 with C held in reserve (Elon, Bezos).

## Conditions (checkable)
1. **Proof sheet, by Nov 1:** one page, three dollar figures (hours saved at loaded rate, revenue or sales lift, cost cut), each sourced to a log or invoice the COO can check. Floor $156k a year ($75 x 2,080 hours). Below $156k, ask below $75. Between $156k and $300k, open on C. Above $300k, a flat open is defensible. No sheet, no meeting.
2. **Meeting by Nov 8:** COO first, CEO second (Wickman). Gratitude, one story, state the ask once, then stop. Never "or else."
3. **Targets:** at most three, hours saved first, written and signed by the COO before $55 starts. Hitting them triggers $75 automatically; no re-vote at day 90 (Leila, Wickman, Martell).
4. **Documentation:** runbooks for the six core systems. By day 30, someone other than the founder runs one system from the runbook; the COO runs one from the document alone by day 60.
5. **Stake:** not in this ask. After the first bonus quarter or day 90, ask for a written share (10 to 15 percent) of measured savings and for rights to the know-how (Naval).

## Next steps
- This week (by Nov 1): build the proof sheet and the three day-90 targets.
- By Nov 8: hold the meeting.
- Day 90: check targets, pay $75.

**Cheapest test:** build the sheet and have the COO initial one line before booking the CEO meeting.
**Kill criteria:** if no figure survives the COO's check, do not ask; build the number first. If both say "not now" with no date, treat that as an answer about the company, and decide privately whether to stay.
